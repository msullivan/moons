Port tides

Tide predictions computed in your browser from the N-body simulation: each moon's real position drives the equilibrium tide, and each port's response — fitted to the 2-D tide map model of the seas — turns it into local high and low water. Heights are metres from mean sea level; times are local zone time.

Year overview

Every port over one year

Daily range is a day's highest minus lowest water; neap and spring are its 10th and 90th percentiles. HW = high waters per day (share of days), counting a high only if it stands 10 cm above the lows either side. The form number is Σ once-daily ÷ Σ twice-daily partial-tide amplitudes: below 0.25 is twice-daily, above 3 once-daily.

Under the Great Western Bridge

The one deep gap through the isthmus, 1.6 km wide and 60 m deep, like the Golden Gate. Top: the water level at either end. Bottom: the current through it, driven by the difference and held back by friction and the losses at the narrows, as in the 2-D model. Positive current runs north, out of the Central Sea.
