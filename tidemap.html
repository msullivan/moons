Tide map

The tide across Qamis, computed live in your browser: the N-body simulation moves the moons, and a 2-D shallow-water model of the seas around the map (invented depths, Coriolis, one gap in the isthmus) responds. Colour is water level relative to mean sea level. Click the sea to chart any point.

Great Western Bridge

—

The one gap in the isthmus, sized like the Golden Gate (1.6 km wide, 60 m deep). Current through it, from the level difference across the isthmus.

Model: ocean_grid.js (mask, invented bathymetry), ocean2d.js (solver), run in tidemap_worker.js. Notes and caveats in TIDES.md (2-D Tide Model). Each new date spins the ocean up for 3 simulated days first.
